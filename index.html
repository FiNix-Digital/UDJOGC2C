The Vision Behind UDJOG

UDJOG C2C Services Pvt. Ltd. was founded with a single, powerful mission: to bridge the gap between India's unemployed youth and the thousands of job opportunities that go unfilled every year.

The name UDJOG — short for "Ur Dream Job is Our Goal" — reflects our core promise. We don't see ourselves as just a placement agency. We are your career partner, committed to your success from the first conversation to your first day at work and beyond.

Under the leadership of Alumalla Sandeep Reddy, UDJOG has grown to become a trusted name across the India, particularly for candidates from rural, semi-urban, and underprivileged backgrounds who often lack access to quality career guidance.

Talent is everywhere, and everyone deserves a chance.

help Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we get asked the most.

Is the placement service free for job seekers?

add

Yes, absolutely! UDJOG C2C Services does not charge job seekers for career counselling, placement support, resume building, or interview preparation. Our services to candidates are completely free of cost. We operate on a placement-fee model with hiring companies.

What kind of training do you offer and is it free?

add

We offer IT and Non-IT training programs covering software testing, MS Office, communication skills, sales techniques, banking basics, and interview preparation. Basic training and skill guidance are provided free. Certain advanced or specialized certification programs may have nominal fees, which will be communicated clearly upfront.

How long does the placement process take?

add

Placement timelines vary by candidate profile and job market conditions. For freshers with complete documentation, we typically schedule interviews within 1–2 weeks. Experienced professionals may find placements faster. The full cycle — from registration to joining — usually takes 2–6 weeks depending on the sector.

Do you offer internship opportunities?

add

Yes! We work with companies that offer internship programs for freshers, particularly in IT, digital marketing, and business operations. Internships are a great way to gain experience and often lead to full-time employment. Reach out to us to know about current internship openings.

What is the eligibility criteria to register with UDJOG?

add

We welcome candidates with qualifications ranging from 10th / SSC pass to Post Graduation. Whether you are a fresher with no experience or an experienced professional looking for a career change, you are eligible to register. There are no strict eligibility barriers — we assess each candidate individually.

Do you cover rural areas and districts outside Hyderabad?

add

Absolutely! Supporting candidates from rural, semi-urban, and tier-2/tier-3 towns across the India is one of our core missions. We actively reach out to candidates in districts outside Hyderabad and provide remote guidance via phone and WhatsApp. Local job opportunities within districts are also sourced where available.
