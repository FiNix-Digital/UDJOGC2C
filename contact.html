Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers to the questions candidates and employers ask us most.

Is your career counselling and job placement really 100% free? expand_more
Yes, absolutely! UDJOG C2C Services does NOT charge any fee from job seekers for career counselling, resume writing, or interview scheduling. We partner directly with companies who sponsor placement drives.
Who can apply through UDJOG? expand_more
Anyone! Candidates with 10th, 12th/Intermediate, Diploma, B.Tech, Degree (B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BA), or Post Graduation (MBA, MCA, M.Tech) are all eligible. We have dedicated openings for freshers as well as experienced candidates.
What kind of skill training do you provide? expand_more
We provide targeted training in spoken English, corporate communication, resume structuring, mock interviews, IT basics (testing, networking, software), customer support protocols, and BFSI operations.
How long does it take to get placed? expand_more
Most active candidates receive interview schedules within 3 to 10 days of completing their profile and preliminary counselling assessment.
I am an employer. How do I post a requirement and hire? expand_more
Employers can submit job openings directly via our Post a Job page. Our recruitment admin reviews your requirement, matches relevant pre-trained candidates, and arranges interview line-ups within 24-48 hours.
